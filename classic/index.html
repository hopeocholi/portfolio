Project Tidewatch

Banks still watch payments with rules that look at one account at a time. Launderers fan money out, gather it back and cycle it between banks. Tidewatch plans a graph-enhanced model to score every payment, and an AI copilot that gathers the evidence and drafts the case while an investigator makes the call. I wrote the PID, run the plan, and co-lead the data engineering with Chinelo Okeke.

Portfolio simulation. Northquay Bank and its committees are fictional. The data is the public, fully synthetic IBM AML collection with no real customers. The documents, plan and code are real work.

Decisions I made, and what they cost

Prove value before buyingOf four options, buying a platform was fastest, but the bank could not yet specify or challenge a vendor model. A governed proof of value builds that capability first.
Start the agent earlyThe copilot build begins in week 11 on the candidate model, taking two weeks off the critical path. Possible rework after validation is named as a risk and owned.
Targets below the ceilingThe published best F1 of 0.63 used a proprietary library. We use open-source code, so the target is 0.55 with a 0.45 threshold.
Human decision by designThe copilot has no tool that closes an alert, files a report or contacts a customer. The constraint lives in the tool set, not in a prompt.

How I work

Four habits that show up on every project

Business case · PID · WBS

Baseline first

Scope, schedule and cost are agreed before anything moves, so every later conversation has something fixed to measure against.

Tolerances · exception reports

Manage by exception

Inside tolerance, I decide. Beyond it, the board hears from me early, with options, before the business feels it.

Earned value · SPI · CPI

Measure progress

Progress is a number with a source. When delivery drifts, the indices show it before anyone has to guess.

Acceptance criteria · sign-off

Make done a fact

Criteria agreed up front turn sign-off into evidence, whether the work is a cutover, a VPN stage or a vendor deliverable.
